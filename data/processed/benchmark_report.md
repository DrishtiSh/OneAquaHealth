# Stage 11 benchmark: the pipeline vs the simulator's ground truth

Simulated data (seed 42): 10 sites x 104 weeks (2024-09-23 to 2026-09-14), 713 citizen reports. Generated by `python -m pipeline.benchmark.benchmark`; do not edit by hand.

## Headline

- **Events found:** 10 of 12 true contamination events (83%); 8 of them at the confirmed level.
- **False alarms:** 6 of 15 reported incidents matched no true event (incident precision 60%).
- **Site-week alerts (normal sensitivity, confirmed or possible):** precision 77%, recall 30% overall and 78% on weeks with at least one report.
- **Entry point:** the top-ranked source was right for 60% of found events.
- **Scores:** water-quality index off by 5.9 points on average; the 90% intervals contained the true W 91% and the true H 87% of the time.

## Caveats

- Measured on simulated data with a known answer. Real-world accuracy is unknown until the pipeline is compared with lab samples or confirmed reports.
- A site-week counts as truly changed when the simulated event pulled W down by more than the detector's own threshold (10 points at normal sensitivity); smaller ripples far downstream are not counted as misses.
- True drops are recomputed from the events with the simulator's decay and travel-lag rules; overlapping events add up and the clip of W at 0 is ignored.
- Incidents are matched to true events when they share a site and their weeks overlap within 1 week.
- Thresholds were fixed before this comparison and were not tuned against it.
